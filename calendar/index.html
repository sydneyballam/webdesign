How and why

One place for it all. Our community shares events in many ways: Facebook posts, flyers in shop windows, chamber and venue websites, and tribal and county calendars. This calendar brings them together, so you can look in one spot instead of many.

Flyers and pictures, in words. Many events are shared as images on Facebook, and the details written inside a picture are often missed by Google. They also can't be copied or added to a phone. Here, each one becomes plain text with the date, time, place and town, so it can be searched, shared and added to your own calendar.

Where events come from. Calendars that local organizations publish are checked automatically every hour. Neighbors and businesses can send an event through the form, and each one is reviewed before it appears. When the same event is listed in several places, it shows up here once, and every event links to where it came from so you can see the original.

Easy to use. It's a simple, fast page with no accounts and no ads. Search by word or town, narrow by date, or enter your ZIP code to see what's close to you. View a list, a month or a map, add a single event to your calendar, share it with a friend, or follow it in your calendar app (an iPhone or a computer) and it updates itself. If something looks off, every event has a link to report a mistake.

How far it reaches. We aim to cover towns within about a 90 minute drive of Willow Creek, including Hoopa, Weaverville, Arcata and Eureka.
